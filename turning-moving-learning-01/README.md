# Turning and Moving Patient — Learning 01
Faculty of Nursing — Tobruk University. Skill 05.

## Coverage
All 17 source entries are retained, in order, in three sequences: supine to side-lying (1–7), supine to prone (8–13), moving toward the side of the bed (14–17). Primary sources are the supplied `5. Turning and Moving Patient-1.docx` and `5. Turning and Moving Patient.docx`. Original English is visible in the guide; Arabic is a teaching translation. Neither document supplies rationales. The app labels its explanations as added teaching explanations and does not award the source checklist's 51-point clinical score.

## Learning features
Articulated mannequin with three-quarter/overhead views, switchable nurse side for near/far orientation, end-view rotation inset, arm and leg preparation, coordinated trunk roll, limb support, and staged lateral translation. Learn, Train, Scenario test, Source guide, and 24-question written practice/review with explanations and missed-question retry. Percentage and active-movement clock refer only to screen interaction. A shared 1–2–3 gate precedes steps 6,12,15,17. First setup choices remain visible after correction. Safety pause invalidates readiness and requires reassessment; it is not treated as a wrong answer.

## Source gaps and safety limits
This is an isolated-segment classroom mannequin illustration, not a complete clinical handling or proning protocol. Step9 says the near arm is placed “under his side” without detail. The guide retains the wording and explains the ambiguity; the renderer does not trap or load the arm. Head protection is maintained during the prone turn as a separately disclosed safeguard, even though source step13 lists the final side-facing head after the turn. Assessment, staffing, equipment, breathing, lines, restrictions, support and bed-safety checks are additions, not attributed to the teaching sheet. No real force, pressure, airway, or patient-specific safety measurement is performed. No universal staffing number, edge distance, movement speed or device technique is prescribed.

## Packaging
This folder is self-contained for static hosting. `boot.js` expands the compact bilingual content and applies deterministic replacements to a pinned local engine copy; it does not modify any prior module. The compiled inline engine is captured by Save offline. Logos load from the local `logos.json` and are embedded in offline exports. There is no login, analytics or persistent student-data storage.
