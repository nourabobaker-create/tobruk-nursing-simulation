## Three-lesson batch — insulin, anticoagulants and opioids

`insulin-safety/`, `anticoagulant-safety/` and `opioid-safety/` each contain a self-contained `index.html` and an independently authored `lesson.json`. Each lesson has five concept sections, three causal error chains and three cases. The batch has nine fictional cases, 54 authored decision nodes, 108 choices and 118 terminating paths. These counts describe authored learning content, not learners or verified competence.

Wrong decisions continue into separate detection, exposure or delay branches; they are not forced retries. Choices lock until the learner follows that path. Debriefs preserve reached-patient exposure even after appropriate escalation. A learner can rewind an earlier decision and compare reviewed paths within the page. Observations are scripted and sourced learning examples, not dose-response calculations, outcome probabilities or predicted timing. Rescue drug dosing and autonomous prescription changes are excluded.

Primary evidence is cited inside each JSON and page: insulin product labels and hypoglycaemia guidance; heparin product labels, medication-transition guidance and NICE head-injury guidance; oral immediate-release oxycodone product labeling, FDA warnings and APSF ventilation-monitoring guidance. All checked 29 October 2026. The tenfold insulin-exposure branch explicitly asks for immediate urgent clinical help. Heparin wrong-product and duplicate branches require recognition and notification before team-management scenes.

The shared authoring source is in `shared/`. Rebuild with `python3 shared/build_lessons.py` and validate with `node shared/branching.test.cjs` from this directory. The test traverses every authored path, checks bilingual fields, evidence IDs, termination and reachability, and exercises selection locking and rewind while preserving exposure history. The built page embeds the data, style and engine so its lesson content works in a downloaded HTML file. Existing optional measurement records starts and completed reviews, including a new start when rewinding a completed path; learner choices and case text are not transmitted.
