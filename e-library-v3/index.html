Topic-based Research Shelf · رف بحثي حسب الموضوع

Faculty members can send a research or teaching topic. The library can then build a focused shelf containing relevant open books/chapters, authoritative guidelines, systematic reviews, recent open-access primary studies, suggested keywords/MeSH terms, and a short reading list. This is intended to support searching and supervision — not to replace a full systematic search when one is required.

Proposed workflow: Topic → key concepts/MeSH → books/guidelines → review evidence → recent primary studies → classified reading shelf → periodic update.
