Reading the language of dosage questions
How many tablets? / How much volume?
The first asks for a tablet count. The second asks for the volume of liquid. Identify the answer unit before calculating.
Prescribed dose / Available strength
The prescribed dose is the amount of drug required. The available strength tells you the amount in each tablet or in a stated volume.
Per dose / Per day in divided doses
“Per dose” applies to one dose. “Per day in divided doses” gives a daily total that you divide by the stated number of doses.
Final volume / Volume of diluent
Final volume is the total volume after reconstitution. Volume of diluent is the amount of liquid added.
Over / Drop factor
“Over” followed by a duration specifies the infusion time. The drop factor is the number of drops per millilitre printed on the tubing package.
