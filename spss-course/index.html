بيانات تدريب تمريضية وهمية · Fictional Nursing Education Dataset

هذه البيانات مُولّدة للتدريب ولا تمثل أي طالب حقيقي. صُممت بحيث يمكنك استخدامها في Frequencies وReliability وChi-square وt tests والارتباط والانحدار.

This dataset is generated for training and represents no real student. It supports practice with frequencies, reliability, chi-square, t tests, correlation and regression.

Variables / المتغيرات:
sex: 1=Female, 2=Male · attended_review: 1=Yes, 0=No · passed: 1=Yes, 0=No · satisfaction_1…5: Likert items (1–5).

Practice missions · مهام التطبيق

  1. Frequencies: sex, attended_review, passed.
  2. Descriptives/Explore: study_hours, stress_score, exam_pre, exam_post.
  3. Reliability: satisfaction_1 to satisfaction_5.
  4. Chi-square: attended_review × passed.
  5. Independent t test: exam_post by attended_review.
  6. Paired t test: exam_pre vs exam_post.
  7. Correlation: study_hours with exam_post.
  8. Optional regression: exam_post predicted by study_hours, sleep_hours and stress_score.
