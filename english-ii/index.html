Learning outcomes

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to:

1 Recognize common wound-assessment terminology.
2 Distinguish wound bed, surrounding skin and exudate.
3 Read a simple wound assessment chart.
4 Organize observations clearly and accurately.
5 Identify incomplete or contradictory documentation.
6 Decide which changes are important to report.
بنهاية الدرس تستطيع التعرّف على مصطلحات تقييم الجروح، تصنيف الملاحظات، قراءة نموذج تقييم مبسط، واكتشاف المعلومات المتناقضة أو الناقصة.
Core idea

Why precise wound description matters

A wound should not simply be described as good, bad, or better. Useful documentation tells the reader what is actually present and makes comparison over time possible.

Too vague: “The wound looks bad.”

More useful: “The wound bed is sloughy. The surrounding skin is inflamed and there is moderate purulent exudate.”

الكلمات العامة مثل “جيد” أو “سيئ” لا تصف الجرح بدقة. الأفضل تسجيل ما شوهد فعلياً: حالة قاع الجرح، الجلد المحيط، وكمية ونوع الإفرازات.
Visual 1

Three areas to observe

Look in an organized way. Wound bed → surrounding skin → exudate. This prevents different findings from being mixed together.
رتّب الملاحظة دائماً: قاع الجرح ← الجلد المحيط ← الإفرازات. هذا يساعد على توثيق واضح.
Vocabulary

Core wound terminology · tap 🔊 to hear English

necrosisdeath of cells or tissue
نخر الأنسجة أو موتها.
necroticdescribing tissue that is dead
صفة للأنسجة الميتة: نَخِري.
escharthick, dry, dark or black necrotic tissue
نسيج نخري جاف داكن أو أسود.
sloughdead tissue that may appear yellowish
نسيج ميت قد يبدو أصفر اللون.
granulation tissuered tissue associated with wound healing
نسيج حُبيبي أحمر يرتبط بعملية التئام الجرح.
maceratedsoftened because of excessive moisture
متليّن بسبب الرطوبة الزائدة.
inflamedshowing inflammation; may be red, warm or swollen
ملتهب؛ وقد يظهر احمرار أو دفء أو تورم.
exudatefluid or discharge from a wound
إفرازات أو سائل يخرج من الجرح.
serousthin, clear or pale-yellow fluid
إفراز مصلي رقيق وشفاف أو أصفر باهت.
haemoserousserous fluid containing some blood
إفراز مصلي يحتوي بعض الدم.
purulentpus-like discharge, often yellow or green
إفراز قيحي يشبه الصديد وقد يكون أصفر أو أخضر.
debridementremoval of dead tissue
تنضير الجرح: إزالة الأنسجة الميتة.
swaba sample collected from a wound
مسحة تؤخذ من الجرح للفحص.
cellulitisinflammation of tissue under the skin, often associated with infection

Wound bed preparation

The TIME framework

LetterFocusMain question
TTissueIs the tissue viable or non-viable?
IInfection / inflammationIs infection or excessive inflammation present?
MMoistureIs the wound too wet or too dry?
EEdgeIs the wound edge progressing toward healing?
TIME: الأنسجة، العدوى/الالتهاب، توازن الرطوبة، وحافة الجرح.
Moisture balance

Too wet vs too dry

Excessive moisture may contribute to maceration of the surrounding skin.

Excessive dryness is called desiccation.

Moisture must be assessed as part of the whole wound picture.

Clinical thinking

Think before you conclude

You know only this:

“The wound is red. The patient reports pain. Moderate exudate is present.”

Can you automatically document: “The wound is infected”?

No. Record the findings you actually have. A conclusion should not be invented from incomplete information. Separate what you observed, what you concluded, and what still needs assessment.
لا تساوِ بين الملاحظة والاستنتاج. سجّل ما شاهدته فعلياً، ولا تفترض نتيجة لم تُثبت.
Spot what is wrong

Find the contradiction

Wound siteLeft ankle
Wound descriptionSloughy
Surrounding skinInflamed
Exudate amountNil
Exudate typePurulent
OdourPresent

What is wrong? If exudate amount is “nil”, a purulent exudate type should not simultaneously be documented without clarification.

Challenge

What information is missing?

“Moderate exudate present.”

The amount is given, but the type of exudate is missing.

“Skin red.”

Clarify that this refers to the surrounding skin and add other relevant observed features if present.

“Wound 4 × 3 cm.”

The measurement is useful, but it does not describe the wound bed, surrounding skin, exudate, odour or other relevant findings.

تعلّم اكتشاف ما هو موجود في التوثيق وما هو ناقص، بدلاً من الاكتفاء بقراءة الجملة كما هي.

Final case

Mr Salem · put the lesson together

Yesterday: granulated wound bed; healthy surrounding skin; small serous exudate; no odour.

Today: granulation remains, a small sloughy area is present, surrounding skin is red and warm, exudate is moderate and purulent, and unpleasant odour is present.

Your task: identify what changed, then write a concise description of today's wound in your notes.

Right lower-leg wound with granulation tissue and a new small sloughy area. Surrounding skin is red and warm. Moderate purulent exudate and unpleasant odour are present. These are changes from the previous assessment and should be documented accurately and communicated according to local practice.
